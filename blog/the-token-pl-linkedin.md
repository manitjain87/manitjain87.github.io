Dylan Patel's firm spends $7M a year on Claude Code. That's north of 25% of salary costs. Twelve months ago the number was in the tens of thousands.

Jensen Huang draws the line from the other side: if a $500K engineer isn't burning $250K in tokens, be "deeply alarmed."

Token spend has arrived in real P&Ls. But the supply side is where it gets interesting.

Anthropic's gross margins went from ~30% to a floor of 72%. That's not cost-cutting — it's operating leverage. AI infrastructure is fixed cost. When token volume explodes, unit costs collapse. And when demand is inelastic — when the alternative to your model is no model — you capture the spread as margin. Same dynamic at Nebius: 71% gross margin, 70% of incremental revenue drops to the bottom line. The token supply chain is starting to look like the chip supply chain.

The piece covers why the token bill only goes one direction (Jevons Paradox with receipts from Gavin Baker, Krishna Rao, and Meta's 60 trillion token month), how Nebius is building the refinery while everyone else is buying crude, and the three questions every CFO needs to answer before the "AI spend optimisation" wave hits earnings calls.
