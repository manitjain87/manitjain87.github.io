Your products are either showing up in AI shopping agents — or they're not. There's no middle ground.

When a customer asks ChatGPT to "find me the best price," one retailer returns 60 products. Another returns: `Reference BOT: 0.561e1202...`

When an AI shopping agent queries a product catalog, it arrives with the same signature as a scraper.  A merchant cannot distinguish OpenAI's Shopping feature — routing a customer ready to buy — from a competitor's price monitor extracting data for arbitrage.

That's the access problem. But access is just one layer. When an AI shopping agent finds your product in a chat interface and routes the customer to checkout, five things had to happen first. Someone had to make your catalog discoverable by agents. Someone had to verify the agent wasn't a scraper. Someone had to authenticate the customer behind it. Someone had to close the payment. Someone had to deliver the goods.

1. Discovery (GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot and Shopify's SimGym LLM personas for agentic A/B testing running 2,000 AI shoppers)
2. Access (Cloudflare processing 241B tokens/week)
3. Credential (Visa, Mastercard, Stripe converging on agent identity)
4. Payment (Stripe + dLocal building parallel rails)
5. Fulfillment (Amazon + MercadoLibre making logistics programmable)

The merchants inside the stack get the queries. The merchants outside get reference codes.
