That decision is being made right now — across five layers of infrastructure most executives aren't tracking.

Someone had to verify the agent wasn't a scraper. Someone had to authenticate the customer behind it. Someone had to close the payment. Someone had to deliver the goods.

Discovery: Shopify is running 2,000 AI shoppers against live storefronts to simulate what agents will do at scale. Access: Cloudflare processed 241 billion tokens through AI Gateway in a single week. Credential: Visa, Mastercard, Amazon, and Stripe are converging on a single agent identity standard. Payment: Stripe and dLocal are building parallel rails for developed and emerging markets. Fulfillment: Amazon and MercadoLibre are making logistics programmable.

This is a distribution decision, not a security decision. The merchants inside the stack get the queries. The merchants outside it don't.
