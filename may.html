Mental Models

Thinking Frameworks

Post-Merger Integration
Map the full stack before you touch anything. Standardise where possible, preserve what works. The integration is not the goal — the operating model is.
Marketplace Economics
Five tenets: merchant-first, healthy unit economics, customer experience, reinvestment, trust. Every marketplace breaks when one of these is neglected.
AI-Native Org Design
SaaS seats become variable AI consumption costs. Headcount decouples from output. The finance function rebuilds around data infrastructure, not spreadsheets.
Turnaround Finance
People-centered transformation. With the right focus, accountability, and data, talented teams deliver results that forecasting models cannot predict.
Capital Allocation
Every dollar has an opportunity cost. The job is not to say no — it is to ensure every yes is the best possible use of scarce capital. Incentives drive outcomes.
Systems Architecture
Finance is infrastructure. Map the full stack. Understand which systems talk to which. Build the architecture before you automate the processes.
